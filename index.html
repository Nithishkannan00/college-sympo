REGULATORY DIRECTIVES

GENERAL RULES

  • Minimum 1 technical event must be selected.
  • Maximum 2 technical events can be selected.
  • The Structura Quest can be selected only if at least one technical event is selected.
  • The Structura Quest requires exactly 2 members.
  • Paper Presentation can have 1, 2, or 3 participants.
  • Same mobile number cannot be used for multiple registrations.
  • Team name must be unique across all registrations.
  • No on-spot registration permitted.
  • Registration closes when the overall symposium capacity is reached.
  • Paper Presentation: Team size 1–3 participants. 13–15 slides. Submit PPTX + PDF backup. File name: Topic Name_College Name. Fonts: Calibri or Times New Roman. Acknowledge sources, avoid plagiarism, and submit before 07.10.2026.
  • AutoCAD: AutoCAD 2026. Individual event. 45 minutes. Topic: Normal Residential Building / Residential Building. Prepare Plan, Elevation, and Sectional View. Proper dimensions, labels, layers, and standards. Filename: CADD.2026.
  • Code Cracking: Individual IS 456:2000 technical quiz. Stage 1 (manual paper MCQ, 25 questions, 15 minutes, closed book, one answer per question). Stage 2 (PPT MCQ, 30 seconds per question). Shortlisted Stage 1 qualifiers proceed to Stage 2.
  • The Structura Quest: Exactly 2 members. Stage 1 — Quiz Battle, Stage 2 — DIZE MISSION (8 challenge formats), Stage 3 — Treasure Hunt (30 mins, strict clue order).
  • Report before the scheduled event time.
  • Follow coordinator and judge instructions at all times.
  • No copying or unauthorized assistance.
  • No malpractice — malpractice results in immediate disqualification.
  • No discussion or communication where strictly prohibited.
  • Follow event-specific restrictions on electronic devices, mobile phones, and smartwatches.
  • Participants must behave responsibly. Unsafe or disruptive behaviour is not permitted.
  • Judges and coordinators' decisions are final.
  • Symposium Date: 10.10.2026 — Saturday
  • Registration Fee: FREE
  • Registration Deadline: 04.10.2026
  • PPT Submission Deadline: 07.10.2026
  • Official PPT Submission Email: structuraaamec@gmail.com
  • Hospitality Notice: Food will be provided for Inter-College participants only.
  • No on-spot registration.
